🧮 How the math works
This page uses the identical math as the Darkmoon VS calculator and your original spreadsheet:

debuff = ⌈ max(0, 1 − (your power × element multiplier) ÷ defender power) ⌉ rounded up to the nearest 5%.
KOs = debuff % × 480 · fodder = KOs ÷ 6 — each 6-KO team applies 1.25% debuff · KOs are capped at 475 (the game's max).

Element advantage ×1.2 · disadvantage ×0.8 (Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water, Water beats Fire).
Special-skill adjustments multiply the KO count up when the defender carries them: 🔄 Life Flip +25% · ⚡ Stun +20% · 💤 Sleep +15% · 💥 6x+ DMG +30% — plus tier disadvantage +15% per tier step.

The Pure · no characters number is the raw power + element answer; the With characters number includes the skill & tier adjustments — exactly like the main calculator's two tiles.
