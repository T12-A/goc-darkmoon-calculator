      MP-aware rotation simulation

      Simulation follows the kiriblog battle model: the attacker enters with MP 4, the first hit lands after their initial TU = ⌊(1000 − Speed) ÷ 10⌋, and every hit after that waits the skill's TU cost before the next attack. Skills spend MP (−MP) or charge it (+MP); each turn the sim picks the best affordable damage skill, and recharges with a +MP skill when dry. Conditions like “Requires > 75% HP” are assumed met — check each skill's notes.

      📐 How the math works

      TU (turn units / true speed) — from the official kiriblog battle guide: a character's initial TU on entering the field is (1000 − Speed) ÷ 10 with fractions rounded down. Lower TU = acts sooner. After attacking, the used skill's TU cost becomes the wait until the next attack.

      Damage per hit = Attacker ATK × skill multiplier × element modifier (×1.2 advantage, ×0.8 disadvantage, ×1 neutral).

      KOs needed = ⌈ Defender HP ÷ damage per hit ⌉ — the number of hits to chip the defender to zero.

      Time to KO = initial TU + (KOs − 1) × skill TU — the first hit is ready after the attacker's true-speed TU; each follow-up waits the skill's TU.

      MP — characters start each entrance with MP 4. Skills with −MP cost it; +MP skills recharge it. The rotation sim spends the best affordable skill and recharges when needed.

      Data — the full Tokyo Debunker character database (257 characters, 1,219 skills) embedded from the published spreadsheet; ATK is shown as its letter grade (P/S) while the numeric ATK input is your real stat.
