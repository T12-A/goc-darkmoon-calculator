🧮 How the math works
This page uses the identical math as the Darkmoon VS calculator and your original spreadsheet:

Every 6-KO team applied to a battle station adds 1.25% debuff.
debuff % = teams × 1.25% · teams = KOs ÷ 6 · KOs = debuff % × 480
The station is defeated when your team power ≥ opponent power × (100% − debuff%) — that final number is the Power Needed to Defeat shown for every row.

KOs are capped at 475 (the game's max, = 100% debuff · 79 teams). Identical to the spreadsheet's AC4:AE table and the 475-row at 100%.
