      🏷️ How the character ratings work
      Every member's best cards from the Darkmoon / Frostheim sheets are matched to the official List of Character Cards and rated with the Tokyo Debunker Full Data system:

      A B C D = Case Tier (A is the best case rating)
      P = P. ATK grade · S = S. ATK grade — the character's attack stat grade
      TU 45 = True Speed / TU — calculated as ⌊(1000 − Speed) ÷ 10⌋, the character's first-action time

      Rows in ● fire ● water ● grass show the member's element and power. No match entries are cards the sheet didn't name fully enough to identify.
      Computes debuff = ⌈ max(0, 1 − (attacker power × element multiplier) ÷ defender power) ⌉ rounded up to the nearest 5%.
      Element advantage ×1.2 · disadvantage ×0.8 · KOs = debuff × 480 · fodder = KOs ÷ 6 — identical math to the spreadsheet's AC4:AE table.
